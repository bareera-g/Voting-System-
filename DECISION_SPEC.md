# Decision Spec: AIQ Card Design Review

**Status:** Ready for Decision Room  
**Template:** Card design review  
**Owner:** Chief Product Officer  
**Facilitator:** Head of Design  
**Window:** 72 hours from open  
**Quorum:** 70% of invited SLT voters  
**Rule:** The vote informs the decision. The owner records Chosen / Hybrid / Reopen.

## Brief

Cerebri AI is integrating Travel category cards into the AIQ platform. These are the live mockups to vote on: two layout families, a customize pattern, an information popup, and the two-tone icon system. Score each board against the rubric, rank them, and leave one sentence on your top choice. A hybrid is allowed (for example: B’s type, D’s popup, E’s icons). After close, the owner records the outcome. Dissent stays in the archive.

## Rubric (1–5)

| Criterion | What good looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Scanability | A T&E user can read the card’s primary fact in two seconds. |
| Information honesty | The card only promises data AIQ can align with confidence. |
| Brand / trust | Finance-grade: calm, precise, not a consumer widget. |
| Density vs. calm | Enough signal for a decision, not a dashboard stuffed into a card. |
| Implementation confidence | We could ship this direction without a platform rewrite. |

## Options

| ID | Label | Job this board must do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Dark overlay family | Photography + white icon chrome on black. Ten variants of density and info. |
| B | Type-led family | The card is recognized by the word Travel — alignment and scale, not the airplane. |
| C | Customize card | Overflow menu: user picks background, icon, and saves. |
| D | Information popup | Keep the face quiet; long description lives in the i-mark popup. |
| E | Two-tone icons | Phosphor-style icon language for Travel and Contract. |

## SLT roster (voters)

- Chief Executive Officer
- Chief Product Officer (decision owner)
- Chief Technology Officer
- Head of Design (facilitator; votes)
- VP Finance / T&E
- VP Customer Success

**Viewer:** broader product and design team may see outcomes after close; they cannot vote.

## Lifecycle

`Draft` → `Open` → `Quorum met` (70%) → `Closed` → owner records outcome → `Archived`

Ballots are named. Tallies stay blind until a voter submits (and fully named for everyone after close). Votes may change until close. After close they freeze. The owner may override the computed winner with a mandatory reason.
